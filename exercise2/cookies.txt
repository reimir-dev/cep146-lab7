
Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients:
- 2 cups flour
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 cup butter
- 2 eggs

Instructions:
1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Mix ingredients together.
3. Place cookie dough on a baking sheet.
4. Bake for 10 minutes.
5. Let cookies cool for 5 minutes before serving.
Tips:
- Use room-temperature butter for better results.
- Chill the dough for 30 minutes before baking.
- Use parchment paper to prevent sticking.

Serving Suggestion:
- Serve cookies with a glass of milk.
